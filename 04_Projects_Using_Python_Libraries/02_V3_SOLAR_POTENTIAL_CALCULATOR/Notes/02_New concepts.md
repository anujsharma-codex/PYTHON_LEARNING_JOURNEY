Some meanings i learnt now-
GHI, DNI, DHI (The Solar Trio)

GHI (Global Horizontal Irradiance)- Total Solar Radiation hitting flat, horizontal on Earth
The sum of Direct sunbeams and the scattered sky lights
the primary metric we use for standard, non tracking rooftop solar panels

DNI (Direct Normal Irradiance)- The sunlight that comes a straight, direct beam from the sun disk to 
a surface kept perpendicular(Normal) to sun
The intense heat during the peak time when sun is overhear, is due to DNI

DHI (Diffuse Horizontal Irradiance)- The sunlight that has scattered by clouds, dust, and atmosphere
coming from all directions of the sky (excluding the direct sun disk).
On an ambient cloudy day, the sun isnt visible (DNI = 0), But its still day. it is because of DHI.

The solar equation= 
GHI = DNI x cos(theta) + DHI
